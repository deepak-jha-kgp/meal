# Meal — product direction

## Promise

Say what you ate. Keep a useful memory of the day without turning food into a
scoreboard.

## Core loop

1. “Paneer bowl with rice and greens.”
2. `log_meal` stores the estimate, daily rollup, and one isolated trigger.
3. `review-on-meal` wakes the specialist.
4. `review_meal` materializes balance, confidence, a gentle next move, and only
   meaningful signals.
5. Telegram confirms; the live app becomes the daily journal.

## Visual character

A modern food journal: midnight aubergine, pale sage paper, tomato-red accents,
large editorial nutrition numerals, and soft qualitative language. Never a
clinical tracker or gamified calorie dashboard.
